Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Richie Studio ("the app", "we") is a private publishing tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner to publish Richie's own videos and images to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and X). This policy explains what the app does with data, including data it receives through the YouTube API Services, the TikTok APIs and the Pinterest API.

YouTube API Services

Richie Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

Richie Studio uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. Only when the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission, the app accesses the account's basic profile (account ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected) and permission to publish the owner's approved videos to that same account, including the caption and the AI-generated-content disclosure. Use of TikTok features is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. TikTok tokens are stored only on the owner's computer; disconnecting TikTok in the app deletes them, and access can be revoked any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

Pinterest

Richie Studio uses the Pinterest API to publish Richie's own Pins to the owner's own Pinterest business account. Only when the account owner signs in with Pinterest and grants permission, the app accesses:

  • The account's basic profile (username and account ID), to show which account is connected.
  • The owner's own boards, to choose - and if needed create - the board Richie's Pins are saved to.
  • Permission to create Pins on that account: the images and videos the owner has approved, with their title, description, alt text and link.
  • The statistics Pinterest reports for those Pins (for example impressions, saves, Pin clicks and video views), in aggregate, to learn which of our own posts perform best and improve future posts.

The app does not access other people's boards or Pins, messages, or personal data about followers or viewers. Pinterest tokens are stored only on the owner's computer; disconnecting Pinterest in the app deletes them, and the owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the apps section of Pinterest's settings. Use of Pinterest features is also subject to Pinterest's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Instagram, Facebook, Threads and X

The app also publishes the owner's approved posts to the owner's own Instagram account, Facebook Page, Threads account and X account, and reads the aggregated statistics those platforms report for those posts. It accesses nothing else on those accounts. Their access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, are deleted when the platform is disconnected in the app, and can be revoked at any time in each platform's own settings. Use of these platforms is subject to their own terms and privacy policies (Meta for Instagram, Facebook and Threads; X Corp. for X).

YouTube: what data the app accesses

Only when the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app accesses:

  • Upload permission for the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload the videos the owner has approved, with their title, description, tags and settings.
  • The channel's own basic information (channel name and ID), to show which channel is connected.
  • The channel's own statistics from YouTube Analytics (for example views, watch time, average view duration and subscribers gained per video), to learn which of our own videos perform best and improve future videos.

The app does not access other people's channels, private messages, contacts, or any data of viewers beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube reports for the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

The data from every connected platform is used only to publish the owner's posts and to measure their performance for the owner. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Every sign-in token (Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Meta and X) and all statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the app's local data folder. They are never sent to any server we operate (we do not operate one) and are only sent to each platform when the app calls that platform's API on the owner's behalf.

Cookies and device data

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking. The app does not store or read cookies or other information on viewers' devices.

Keeping and deleting data

Disconnecting any platform in the app deletes its stored token immediately, and its statistics are deleted from the app within 30 days.

Statistics received from the YouTube API are refreshed regularly and are deleted from the app within 30 days if the YouTube connection is removed. Disconnecting YouTube in the app deletes the stored sign-in token immediately. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your Google account at Google security settings; after revoking, the app can no longer access your channel.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
