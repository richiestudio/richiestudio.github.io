About this app

Richie Studio is the private publishing tool behind the Richie channels. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by the owner. With the owner's permission it uploads the finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services and to the owner's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, and reads the owner's own statistics so it can learn which videos work best. It does not access anyone else's account or data. Read the privacy policy and the terms.

Facts in our explainers come from published sources and are checked before posting. Pictures and narration are made with AI tools and labelled as AI-made where the platforms ask for it; real photos are only used when their licence allows it, and are credited.
